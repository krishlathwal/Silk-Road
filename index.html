Why sourcing goes wrong

China is a goldmine — and a minefield.

The prices and the range are unbeatable. The hard part is knowing who to trust — and what'll actually turn up.

01

Who's actually a factory?

Sourcing sites are full of traders posing as factories — and from Sydney, it's near impossible to tell the difference.

02

Quality roulette

The sample's perfect. Then the container lands and box 40 isn't. Without an inspection before shipping, you find out when it's already yours.

03

Lost in translation

A spec that was 'confirmed' at 11pm your time has a way of changing by production. Small misunderstandings become expensive cartons.

04

MOQs & logistics

Minimums, freight, customs, GST. None of it is hard once you've done it fifty times. Most buyers are doing it for the first time, alone.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What are the minimum order quantities?+
Depends on the product and the factory. Working across a network means we can often find a maker with a lower minimum than you'd get going direct. Tell us your numbers and we'll tell you what's realistic — even when the answer is 'not yet'.
How does pricing work — and why not go direct?+
One clear landed price, covering sourcing, quality control, freight and customs — no surprise fees. You can absolutely go direct; you'll just also be doing the vetting, the QC, the freight and the customs paperwork yourself, and wearing the cost when something slips. We do all of it, on real factory relationships, so you sit closer to true factory cost without the risk.
What are typical lead times?+
As a rough guide: samples in 1–2 weeks, production 3–5 weeks, plus shipping. We'll give you real dates before you commit — not after.
How do you check quality?+
You approve a physical sample first. We then oversee production and inspect the goods in China before they ship, so defects are caught before they ever reach Australia.
Can I get a custom product or a sample?+
Yes. Full OEM/ODM — your colours, sizes, logo, fabric and packaging — and you sign off a physical sample before any bulk order goes ahead.
What about shipping, customs and GST?+
We arrange freight and customs clearance and deliver to your door. GST applies on imports, and plenty of goods come in duty-free under ChAFTA — we'll walk you through exactly what applies to your order.
Is it safe? How do payments work?+
You're dealing with an accountable Australian contact, not a seller who vanishes when the goods ship. Payment is staged, agreed in writing, before anything begins.
